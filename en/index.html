About

I am Janet Chen, a licensed consultant with 10 years of experience in Hong Kong insurance and family financial planning. In 2016, as a mainlander who came to Hong Kong, I chose to put down roots here and devote myself to helping families protect their future.

Over the past decade, I have earned the insurance industry's MDRT (Million Dollar Round Table) and the higher-tier COT (Court of the Table) distinctions year after year — the truest recognition of my clients' long-term trust.

My clients are mainly mainland families and mainlanders living in Hong Kong. Like me, they work hard here, carrying both hopes for the future and concern for their loved ones. I understand the weight of that concern, so my work never stops at "selling a policy" — it starts from the perspective of the whole family:

  • ▸ Review the family's overall assets and build a balance sheet and cash flow statement
  • ▸ Design a personalised asset allocation plan based on the family's life stage
  • ▸ Make every dollar clearly serve the family's goals for a happy life

"True financial planning means making money serve your life, not making your life revolve around money. My hope is that every client can walk towards a warm and happy life on a clear financial map."
